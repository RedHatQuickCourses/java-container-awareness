To begin this lab, you will need a standard OpenShift Container Platform environment with permissions to deploy applications using StatefulSets, Pod YAML, or standard Deployments. You may use any OpenShift cluster you have access to. However, if you do not currently have access to an environment for this hands-on exercise, follow these instructions to order a lab through the Red Hat Demo Platform, or RHDP.

First, log in to the RHDP portal. Once you are logged in, search the catalog for the entry titled "Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform Cluster (Multi-Cloud)." Select that entry from your search results, and then click the "Order" button.

On the order form, you will need to fill in several required details. For the "Activity" field, select "Practice / Enablement." For the "Purpose," choose "Learning about the product." Set your "Cloud Provider" to "cnv," and select your desired OpenShift version, such as version 4.21. For the cluster size, select "sno," which stands for Single Node OpenShift.

Before finalizing your order, please review the warning at the bottom of the form. You must check the box confirming that you understand these warnings before you can proceed. Finally, click the "Order" button to submit your request.

There are a few important things to keep in mind regarding your lab environment. First, it may take approximately five minutes for the lab to become ready for use. Once it is prepared, you will receive an email containing your access details. You can also retrieve these access details at any time directly through the RHDP portal.

To access your lab via the portal, click on "Services" in the left-hand menu, then select your lab from the list on the right-hand side of the page to view your connection information.

For reference, Red Hat associates can access the portal at demo.redhat.com, while Red Hat partners should use partner.demo.redhat.com.
