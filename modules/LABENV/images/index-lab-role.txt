To get started with your training, you will need to set up your lab environment using the Red Hat Online Learning, or ROLE, platform. Please follow these steps to launch your lab:

First, log in to the ROLE platform. You can find the appropriate portal links for Red Hat associates and partners in the resource section of this course.

Once you are logged in, search the catalog for the entry labeled "FIXME Lab Catalog Name." When you see it in your search results, click the "Launch" button.

From the course page, select the "Lab Environment" tab, and then click the "Create" button to begin the launch process. 

Please wait a few moments for the system to complete the lab creation. Once it is ready, refer to the "SSH Private Key & Instructions" section provided on the page. These details contain everything you need to access your lab environment. 

If you need to access the portal again, remember that Red Hat associates should use the internal role dot r-h-u dot red hat dot com address, while Red Hat partners should use the standard role dot red hat dot com portal.
